The useful bits

A few things
you might wonder.

Are these real tours? +

No. Siam Drift is a portfolio concept. Tour descriptions, pricing, and booking interactions are sample content for demonstration.

Can I book a real boat through this site? +

No. The booking page demonstrates a possible customer journey only. It does not check availability, send a reservation, or accept payment.

How are prices calculated? +

The displayed prices are illustrative group prices stored in local JavaScript data. A real service would confirm prices and availability on a server.

What if the weather changes? +

A real operator should publish a clear weather, rescheduling, and cancellation policy before customers book.

What policies would a real site need? +

At minimum: cancellation and refund terms, privacy policy, terms of service, safety guidance, accessibility information, and clear details about what's included.
